# Bella Vista Italian Kitchen — Location & Parking

## Address
- **Street**: 742 Evergreen Promenade, Suite 100
- **City & State**: San Francisco, CA 94102
- **Phone**: (415) 555-0192

## Parking Information
- **Complimentary Valet Parking**: Available Thursday through Sunday evening from 5:00 PM until closing at our front entrance.
- **Self-Parking Structure**: Evergreen Center Garage directly across the street (entrance on 8th Ave). We offer 2-hour validated parking for dining patrons.
- **Street Parking**: Metered street parking available along Evergreen Promenade and surrounding avenues; meters expire at 6:00 PM.

## Public Transit
- **Metro / Subway**: Located 3 blocks east of the Civic Center BART / Muni Station.
- **Bus**: Lines 5, 21, and 49 stop directly at the corner of Evergreen Promenade & 8th Street.

## Accessibility
- Full ADA compliant entrance, dining floor, and restrooms. Wheelchair accessible elevator to the mezzanine patio.
