# Bella Vista Italian Kitchen — Menu & Culinary Offerings

## Philosophy
Authentic rustic Italian cooking with hand-rolled pasta, imported San Marzano tomatoes, fresh buffalo mozzarella from Campania, and prime seasonal ingredients.

## Antipasti & Starters
- **Burrata Pugliese**: Heirloom tomatoes, basil oil, aged balsamic glaze, grilled sourdough. ($18)
- **Calamari Fritti**: Wild calamari, crispy artichokes, Calabrian chili aioli. ($19)
- **Carpaccio di Manzo**: Prime beef tenderloin, shaved Parmigiano-Reggiano, capers, arugula, white truffle oil. ($21)

## Handmade Pasta (Freshly Made In-House Daily)
- **Tagliatelle al Ragù Bolognese**: Traditional slow-braised veal, beef, and pork ragù with 24-month Parmigiano. ($27)
- **Pappardelle al Cinghiale**: Tuscan wild boar ragù, juniper berries, fresh herbs. ($29)
- **Cacio e Pepe**: Tonnarelli pasta, toasted Tellicherry black peppercorns, aged Pecorino Romano. ($24)
- **Lobster Ravioli**: Maine lobster filling, sherry saffron cream sauce, cherry tomatoes. ($34)

## Wood-Fired Neapolitan Pizzas (Cooked at 900°F)
- **Margherita D.O.P.**: San Marzano tomatoes, buffalo mozzarella, fresh basil, extra virgin olive oil. ($20)
- **Diavola**: Spicy soppressata, crushed red pepper, fior di latte, hot honey drizzle. ($23)
- **Tartufata**: Fontina, wild mushrooms, fresh thyme, shaved black summer truffles. ($26)

## Secondi & Main Courses
- **Bistecca alla Fiorentina**: 32 oz prime dry-aged Porterhouse for two, rosemary sea salt, roasted garlic. ($95)
- **Branzino al Forno**: Whole roasted Mediterranean sea bass, lemon herb gremolata, blistered tomatoes. ($38)
- **Pollo al Mattone**: Crispy brick-pressed organic half chicken, salsa verde, Yukon gold potatoes. ($31)

## Dietary Accommodations
- **Gluten-Free**: Gluten-free handmade penne and wood-fired pizza crusts available upon request.
- **Vegetarian & Vegan**: Multiple plant-based options clearly marked, with dairy-free cashew mozzarella available.
- **Nut Allergies**: Our kitchen is sensitive to nut allergies; please notify the server upon seating.

- **Torta al Cioccolato**: Warm flourless chocolate hazelnut cake with Fior di Latte gelato. ($13)
- **Artisanal Gelato & Sorbetto**: Pistachio, Stracciatella, blood orange, Meyer lemon. ($10)
- Full espresso bar featuring Lavazza Gran Riserva.
