# Bella Vista Italian Kitchen — Restaurant Policies

## Dress Code
- **Standard**: Smart casual dining attire.
- We kindly ask guests to refrain from wearing athletic tank tops, beachwear, flip-flops, or promotional clothing in the main dining room. Collared shirts or smart casual tops are encouraged for evening dinner service.

## Reservation & Cancellation Policy
- **Standard Bookings**: Reservations can be made up to 30 days in advance for parties of up to 10 guests.
- **Grace Period**: We hold reserved tables for 15 minutes past the booking time before releasing them to waitlist guests.
- **Cancellations**: We appreciate at least 2 hours advance notice for standard cancellations. There is no cancellation fee for standard dining reservations.
- **Large Parties (6+ Guests)**: Require a credit card on file. Cancellations with less than 12 hours notice may incur a $25/person fee.

## Corkage & Cakeage
- **Corkage Fee**: $35 per 750ml bottle for bottles not represented on our current wine list, with a maximum of two bottles per table.
- **Cake Cutting / Plating**: $5 per person fee for bringing external celebratory cakes.

## Children & Families
- Children and families are warmly welcomed. High chairs and booster seats are available on a first-come basis.
- A customized bambino menu (pasta pomodoro, crispy chicken cutlet, mini margherita pizza) is available for young diners under 12.

## Private Dining & Special Events
- The **Tuscan Cellar Room** accommodates up to 24 guests for seated private dinners with custom multi-course pairing menus.
- The **Sunlit Pergola Patio** accommodates up to 45 guests for cocktail receptions and milestone gatherings.
- For private event inquiries, please ask for our Events Director at events@bellavistakitchen.com.
